Educational Summary

Understanding these concepts could save you thousands of real dollars.

The House Always Wins

House Edge

Every casino game is mathematically designed so the house keeps a percentage of every bet — this is called the "house edge." Over thousands of bets, this edge guarantees the casino makes money, even if individual players occasionally win. No strategy can overcome a negative expected value.

Average House Edge: 2% – 15%

Gambler's Fallacy

Psychology

The Gambler's Fallacy is the mistaken belief that past random events influence future ones. If a coin lands heads 10 times in a row, people believe tails is "due" — but each flip is completely independent. Casinos exploit this thinking to keep players betting.

Reality: Each event is independent

Sunk Cost Trap

Behavioral Economics

The Sunk Cost Fallacy makes people continue a losing behavior because they've already invested money or time. "I've lost $500, I need to win it back" — this thinking leads to chasing losses and spiraling deeper into debt. Money already lost is gone; continuing to play only increases losses.

Outcome: Deeper losses guaranteed
